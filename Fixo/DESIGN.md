# FIXO — Cinematic Warmth

User-approved palette replacement, 9 November 2026. Editable reference: https://www.figma.com/design/xAym32AGTmERySxtIyBbob

## Atmosphere
Considered home care. Cinematic through composition, warm directional lighting and a calm hierarchy, not constant movement or exaggerated claims. Balanced density, restrained motion, generous desktop spacing and practical mobile controls.

## Palette
Charcoal #17191C for dark canvas. Ink #24262B for primary light-mode text and buttons. Ivory #F6F2EB for light canvas. Champagne #C6A779 for the single accent, paired with dark text. Elevated charcoal #22252A, white #FFFFFF cards, warm gray #69645F secondary light text, #B8B2A9 dark secondary text. Semantic error/success colors remain distinct. No pure black, neon green or purple glows.

## Typography
Manrope for English and Cairo for Arabic, preserving bundled fonts. Body at least 14–16sp. Fluid headlines 30–52sp, restrained weights and generous line height. No decorative all-caps technical copy in product flows. Arabic is authored, not reversed English.

## Components
48px minimum interactive targets. Buttons have clear disabled, pending, error and focus states. Cards 16–24px radius, shallow tinted shadows, no stacked glass borders. Navigation stays fixed and predictable; never drag-to-accidentally-navigate. One primary action per panel. Location has one canonical selector and manual choice remains available without permission.

## Composition
Mobile: compact header, area/search, editorial introduction, service categories and real offerings; no large decorative obstacle before booking. Desktop: constrained 1280–1400px content, asymmetric editorial hero, generous services grid. Tablet intermediate layout. Forms remain readable and bounded on large screens. Bottom bars include safe-inset spacing and never hide the last item.

## Motion
Short 180–350ms opacity/translation transitions. Respect reduced motion and lifecycle/visibility. No infinite foreground animation as a default. Decorative illustrations are never presented as GPS, payment authorization or identity verification.

## Truthfulness
No invented ratings, service counts, trust badges, card authorizations or identities. No 0ms/120fps claims without measurements. Unavailable functionality is labeled or hidden. All services remain free of paid integrations unless explicitly authorized later.
